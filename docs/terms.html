Terms of Service

Lybloo Autopost is an internal tool operated by Lybloo (lybloo.com). It is used only to prepare and share Lybloo's own product content on Lybloo's own social media accounts, and is not available to anyone else.

Use

The tool creates product videos from lybloo.com and sends them to the connected TikTok account as drafts. Nothing is published without the account owner reviewing and posting it in TikTok.

Platform rules

All content shared with the tool must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Changes and ending use

Lybloo may change or stop the tool at any time. Access can be removed in TikTok's app permissions settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent through lybloo.com.
